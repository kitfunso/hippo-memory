### Added

- **`serve()` takes a `keyQuota` hook that can refuse a request with 429.** It is asked once per request, after the per-caller rate limit admits it, on gated /v1 rows (not the webhook-signature rows), add-on routes, POST /mcp and GET /mcp/stream. It is never asked for the local CLI key, the stream heartbeat or the auth recheck. The call holds `tenantId`, a frozen copy of the actor, `method`, `path` without its query, and `requestId`. A refusal `{ retryAfterSec, message }` with a finite `retryAfterSec` above 0 and a non-empty message answers 429 with that text and a `Retry-After` of `retryAfterSec` rounded up, at most 86400. A throw or any other answer admits the request and logs one error line at most once a minute, because a quota guards load, not access. The hook is synchronous. `hippo-memory/server` exports the types `KeyQuota`, `KeyQuotaCall` and `KeyQuotaRefusal`.
