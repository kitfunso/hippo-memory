### Added

Valid applies carry the failure chain (`stored`, `shown`, `followed`, `captured`), and A2 and A5 records count the hippo rows the hook injected by source. Each valid cell writes a git bundle, reader diffs without instruction files, `grade.json` and, for applies, the surface text to `<out>/grading/`.
