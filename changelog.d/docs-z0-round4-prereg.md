### Documentation

- **Z0 round 4 pre-registration (`docs/evals/2026-11-10-z0-round4-prereg.md`).** Round 4 tests whether Sonnet 5.5 with hippo matches Opus 5.5 with built-in memory on tasks solved and repeat mistakes at a lower cost per solved task, with Sonnet alone as the gate that keeps a cheaper model from winning on its own. It reruns round 3's 29 repositories on a new branch cut from round 3's frozen tree plus the harness changes in its items 1 to 10: an arm table, the new tests and their verdict rule, cost per solved task, one pinned model and effort per arm, a G1 check of every request, prices keyed by model, one venv for each Python task, and the new sizer and Level check. No session runs before the founder approves the pilot budget, and no scored session runs before the founder approves the scored budget.
