### Changed

- **A test waits for an event, never a long timer.** Every async child a test starts runs through `tests/_helpers/started-child.ts`: each wait is on a printed line or the exit, with its own deadline, and the whole process tree is stopped through `killTree` before the test's folder is removed. A wait over 10 s must give a reason and none may pass 45 s; `scripts/check-test-env.mjs` and `tests/scripts/own-timeouts.slow.test.ts` fail on any own timeout over 10 s in the default run. Detached session-end workers are recorded by pid, and a test waits for exactly the workers it started to exit before it removes their folder. The slow-test budget drops from 180 s to 30 s. No change to the published package.
