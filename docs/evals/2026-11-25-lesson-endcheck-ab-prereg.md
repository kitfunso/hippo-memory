# Does the end-of-task rule check prevent omission failures on Z0 first applies?

Registered 25 November 2026 before a live smoke or scored launch. Prepared for review on `docs/lesson-experiments`. The experiment directory is `docs/evals/lesson-endcheck-ab`. ## Question and decision

H1 predicts that enabling the slice 1 Stop hook lowers the proportion of omission lessons failed before a human correction. H2 predicts that the hook introduces few failures on tasks that round 3 A2 passed. A lesson fails when the frozen lesson checker says `fail` or `na`, round 3's violation coding. Task acceptance, final-after-correction performance and reminders issued are different outcomes. None substitutes for H1.

The direction in the experiment brief supplies the kill rule for this slice. If ON's omission failure rate is equal to or above OFF's, kill the proposed benefit. If lower and the one-sided paired randomisation p value is at most 0.05, report a reduction on these cases. Otherwise report inconclusive. A reduction alone earns no safety claim. The harm margin chosen for this design is 5 percentage points. Report the observed excess and the one-sided 95% upper limit on new failures among historical passes. Call the result within margin only when that limit is below 5%. Even a clean trial cannot establish zero harm.

The brainstorm's section 6 supplies the design principle of a declared kill result. Its knockout threshold belongs to the knockout experiment. The specific direction here comes from `../briefs/ab.md` in the scratchpad. The report format follows the pre-launch sections of `git -C C:/Users/skf_s/hippo show origin/docs/z0-round4-record:docs/evals/2026-11-10-z0-round4-prereg.md`. No round 4 outcome enters the design.

## Population and allocation

One paired unit holds the same historical first-apply lesson, task, seed history, baseline memory and fresh repetition in both arms. Build the candidate list with experiment 1's `loadRound3`, `firstApplies(filtered,'A2')` and its frozen lesson labels. Include every omission, passing or failing, plus every other historical pass as a safety control. Historical status selects the stated strata only. OFF is always a new live session.

`node register.mjs` rebuilds the manifest and verifies existing content byte for byte. From 148 first-apply lessons, 46 were omissions and 131 were passes. Excluding the 8 non-omission failures leaves 140 candidates. Two pre-treatment snapshots have missing bytes whose hashes could not be reproduced, q256-f1 (project memory) and q256-f5 (native memory). The manifest records their session IDs. This leaves 45 omission cases, including all 9 historical failures, plus 93 non-omission controls. Of the eligible cases, 129 passed historically. This is a fixed historical workload. Effects on a newly sampled repository, an unseen lesson or a different model require another experiment.

The first block includes all 138 cases. Repeat the omission stratum in four blocks total. Use only the first block for historical-pass safety, so the secondary measure gives a historical pass the same weight across lesson types. In each pair, fresh slots have independent homes, sessions and workspaces. Prepare both before either sees the task. Compare their pre-session Git trees and their installed settings after removing Stop and normalising the slot paths. Start them serially. The first byte of SHA-256 of the study name, `/coin/` and the case/repetition key selects ON-first for an odd byte. Sort pairs by SHA-256 of the study name, `/order/` and key. The seed string, delimiters and case IDs are in `paths.mjs`, `design.mjs` and the frozen manifest. This fixes order before responses and spreads time and prompt-cache effects across arms. EVAL_SEED labels the historical sequence. Claude Code supplies fresh samples with no deterministic sampling-seed control.

## Why this size

`node design.mjs` prints the replay cells, assumptions, required pairs and achieved power. The underlying file is `experiment1/data/end-check-prod-replay.json`. The production replay listed 7 of 9 omitted lessons, and listed the target on 0 of 37 omission passes and 0 of 131 passes across all lesson types. There were 24 other-rule listings at first applies. Across the larger replay it listed something in 164 of 1,077 sessions (15.2%). The denominators differ. The latter figure estimates reminder burden. It cannot supply the failure-reduction effect.

For planning, suppose half the reachable omissions are repaired. The working net reduction is 0.5 × 7/46, or 7.61 points. Allow 4% OFF-pass/ON-fail discordance from run-to-run variation or harm, implying 11.61% OFF-fail/ON-pass discordance to retain that net effect. Both assumptions are design choices. Independent paired runs, stable discordance and no additional clustering give 80% power at 180 omission pairs. The calculation sums binomial probabilities over the number of discordant pairs and the exact one-sided sign test's rejection region. The 16 eligible omission cases on seed 1 give 1.9% power. One pass over all 45 gives 21.0%. Four passes give 80.1%. Baseline concentration by family, fewer repairs, correlated model outputs or more reverse discordance can reduce power. Baseline coverage alone supplies no guarantee of efficacy. We freeze four passes without an interim sample-size change. Repetitions improve precision on this finite workload. They do not add lesson families.

Total planned subscription use is **546 real Opus apply sessions**, comprising 180 omission pairs and 93 additional safety pairs. No live teach, preparation, grading or raw API model call is needed for the full trial. Claude Code can make multiple internal turns within an apply. The optional registration smoke is one additional ON apply.

## Measurement and exclusions

Grade both first tries after any Stop-induced continuations and before any human feedback. Use the frozen Z0 command log (main and subagents), the observed pre/post Git trees and the original checker. The outcome assessor uses the same function and inputs in both arms. Pair gains count OFF fail / ON pass. Pair harms count OFF pass / ON fail. The primary estimate is (harms minus gains) / omission pairs. The exact one-sided sign test conditions on gains plus harms. Its inference is conditional on this set of tasks and independent order assignments under the sharp null. Show family-level counts to expose concentration.

For historical passes, report both live failure counts, gains and harms. The upper bound on the harm proportion uses exact one-sided binomial inversion over distinct historical cases, under independent trials. This is conservative for the net excess. Correlated families limit its interpretation as a population bound. Also report sessions receiving a target reminder, false target reminders, and other-rule reminders separately. A target matches the frozen current lesson's key phrase or the leading normalised rule text, as in the replay. A false target listing means the checker already returned pass or inapplicable on the live command history and tree at that Stop. A historical pass can legitimately omit the action this time. A final pass after the reminder can be a repair. Neither alone makes a listing false. Other rules are counted without assigning them the target's grade. Multiple listings count once per session in each rate.

The ON shim executes the installed production command once, freezes the tree and commands only when it returns context, and forwards its stdout unchanged. Stamped events and delivered Stop attachments must agree. The extra time to record an emitted reminder is part of the measured apparatus. An ordinary silent Stop and an ignored reminder are retained. Missing evidence, a checker error, setup failure, interrupted or rate-limited process, unreadable transcript, or a missing assigned arm leaves an incomplete pair. No replacement based on outcomes, no dropping a failed or silent ON run, and no efficacy verdict with missing pairs. A restart skips completed pairs and refuses to overwrite a partial pair. Preserve failed attempts; any recovery protocol that would rerun a dispatched session needs a dated pre-unblinding amendment. Report infrastructure failures by arm. The smoke is a feasibility check only and cannot change membership or be promoted to a scored case.
