# Z0 round 2: hippo against built-in memory on fresh repositories. Pre-registration

**Status: draft.** The fields marked TBD are filled at the freeze tag. This file is fixed in one commit after the tag and before shortlist batch 2 opens. After that commit, a change is an amendment, dated, with what it changes and what had been seen.

The plan behind this file: `docs/evals/2026-10-18-z0-round2-plan.html`.

## Why a round 2

- **The pilot cannot answer H1.** The rustfs pilot is invalid on G2 (Amendment 4): A4 against A0 on repeat mistakes read -0.667, interval [-1, 0], on 6 units in one repository. Its families are spent (Amendment 3, item 4).
- **Most screened rules were rules agents follow anyway.** Stage 3 kept 15 of 33 families. In 17 of the 18 drops, A0 broke the rule at most once in 4 attempts.
- **The go's design was underpowered.** The sizer raised the noise mean square to its bound before it subtracted it, so zero family and sequence estimates kept zero bounds (`z0-size-sim.mjs`, fixed in `ca92f0f5`). At the record's bounds the go's 10-repository design has H1 power 0.146, not the 0.52 its sizes file shows.
- **Hippo changes.** Prereg 240: a hippo change after the freeze needs a new registration.

## What carries over

The 2026-09-29 design and Amendments 1 to 4 hold, except where this file says otherwise. That covers: arms A0, A1, A2, A4 and A5; the session protocol, with the teach messages word for word (prereg 105-109); the definitions; gates G1 to G5; the measurements and the failure chain; the units, the bootstrap and the fixed-repository t; blind analysis; and what gets published.

## What changes

1. **Scope.** H1 and the H4 harm gate, on sets R and N. H3 is computed and reported as the prereg writes it. Set X and H2 do not run. The frozen analyzer runs Holm over H1 and H2 with an H2 not run as NaN (`z0-hypotheses.mjs:154-158`), so H1 is judged at an effective two-sided 0.025. G2 reads only its Claude Code half (`z0-gates.mjs:28-35`). Hippo counts as working only if H1 is a win under both codings and A2 beats A5 (prereg 148).
2. **The hippo build.** Hippo is frozen at tag `z0-r2-freeze` (TBD: commit, dist hash) after the dev rounds below. The runner stays at `45b57c6b` plus `00ce7f4a` (branch `z0/runner-unc`), which folds an agent's `\\server\share` path as written instead of asking the network; it changes no record of a run that ran to its end. Only the hippo build under test and its pins change. The tag commit merges the kept build, `z0/runner-unc` and `feat/z0-fixed-repo`, whose analyzer and sizer fixes are not in `45b57c6b`; that merge changes only `z0-analyze`, `z0-filters`, `z0-gates`, `z0-seed-rule` and the `z0-size` scripts, never `src/` or the files that run a session.
3. **Dev rounds, before the tag.** Every family that exists before the tag is dev or burned (the list below). A round picks the weakest chain link on the tune half and judges a candidate on the check half only. The candidate and the best build run A2 on the same seeds; the best build's A1 and A5 are the comparators. A candidate is kept when, under both codings, its paired gain over the best build is larger than one standard error, its A2 against A5 gain does not shrink, and the paraphrase check passes. At most 3 rounds, and the rounds stop after 2 rounds with no kept change. Each round is logged in `z0-scored-set-record.md` with every candidate, kept or dropped, and its numbers.
4. **The teach-template ban.** `git diff z0-freeze z0-r2-freeze -- src/` holds none of "Please fix it", "Yes, keep doing that" and "because". A match blocks the tag. The paraphrase check (`C:/z0-runs/dev/capture-probe.mjs`) builds each dev lesson's teach message in the runner's two forms and in two reworded forms, and a kept capture change must store the key phrase from the reworded forms as often as from the runner's.
5. **Repositories.** Fresh repositories come only from shortlist batch 2 (`C:/z0-runs/scored-shortlist/b2/candidates.json`: 50 kept of 189 screened). Batch 1, its 10 gated repositories and the dev repositories (cj, bm, hp, ml, ff and the stage 3 set) are excluded. The tuner reads no batch 2 rule text before the tag; on 2026-10-18 only its names and rule counts were listed. The gate target is 25 gated repositories (in stage 3, 4 of 10 gated reached 3 kept families). Batches screen on until the target is met or the authoring budget ends.
6. **Authoring.** The inventory brief is `C:/eval-authors/r2-inventory-template.md` (sha256 `8cf451b3e6d23e380903c3ecfce6c763663e957addd0fb54d8d75cc06a46b4fa`): the stage 3 brief (its sha256 is pinned at `z0-scored-set-record.md:36`) plus criterion 7, a rule against the default an agent would follow with no rule file. Criterion 7 is judged from the code, not from any arm, so it favours neither A1 nor A2. The two-thirds maintainer floor (prereg 51) still holds. Every author stays blind to hippo (prereg 58-64).
7. **The A0 probe.** Before the screen, `C:/z0-runs/scored/probe.mjs` (sha256 `55b757ffb6f3096ebf833c465d3975b35c6547a3069390e7b54a794384928671`) runs A0 alone on each family's screen task, on seeds 1 and 2. A family with no break is dropped; one break sends it to the screen. The screen is unchanged: 4 A0 and 2 A4 sessions, kept on at least 2 A0 breaks and 2 A4 follows (prereg 66-68). Probe sessions never count toward the screen.
   **The leak probe.** Also before the screen, `C:/z0-runs/scored/entangle.mjs` (sha256 `985e6a00ea9fab61b96f01488484ac6d2a5351420c0b9dfb963755084f6f5d47`) runs each lesson's teach task alone in A1 and in A2 on the frozen build, each in a fresh run, on seeds 1 and 2. G3's own scan (`storedAt`, `leaks.mjs:133`) then reads every memory surface, instruction file and hippo store the session left for the key phrase of every other lesson in its sequence, a reversal's root excepted (as `assertNoPhraseLeaks`). A2 is in the probe because the leak below came from A2's own notes: in the sessions where cj-f3 was taught before cj-f2, Claude Code's memory held cj-f2's key phrase in 3 of 3 A2 sessions and in 0 of 5 in A1, A4 and A5, and a first probe in A1 alone missed it. A hit means that lesson's teach voids its sequence and seed in every arm whenever it runs before the other lesson's teach. In the cj-c1c dev run, cj-f3's teach (breaking changes) left cj-f2's key phrase "Conventional commits" in Claude Code's own memory, and 2 of 3 seeds were voided. A hit sends the other lesson's key phrase back to its blind author for a new pick, and the pair is probed again; a second hit drops that lesson's family. A lesson whose attempts did not all run is undecided and is probed again. The convention-pair reading (`z0-scored-set-record.md`, "Convention pairs") still runs first; cj predates it, and the leak probe tests that reading against what the agent writes. The family brief (`C:/eval-authors/s3x-family-template.md`, sha256 `617e651d6638641c66b78e14f2b8b82bfee4b10595c102247f43ea777666f81b`) now also bars a key phrase that names a convention, standard or tool two lessons share. Leak-probe sessions count in the authoring budget, never toward the screen.
8. **Sizing and the seed rule.** A cap miss no longer stops the run. When wave 1 asks for more than 9 seeds, that hypothesis runs at 9 (`--on-miss cap`, added in `295bd467` and the default of `z0-size.mjs` and `z0-seed-rule.mjs` since `bf6dfe6c`). Under the stop rule, a run that stops scores nothing, and the runs that go on are the ones whose wave 1 underestimated the spread, so the level check failed. On the stage 2 calibration for sets R and N (cj and bm), at a 15-point minimum effect, H1 power at the bounds with 25 repositories of 2 families is 0.867 (0.514 under the stop rule). At 100,000 level simulations (seed 1), the level check holds: its worst cell, the rule at the bounds, reads 0.0257 against a ceiling of 0.0260, an upper bound of about 0.0267. The stop rule reads 0.0305 against 0.0263 there, with 39% of runs stopped (`C:/z0-runs/r2-size/sizes-rn-cap-l100k.json`, `sizes-rn-stop-25-l100k.json`). The fixed sizer runs again on that calibration plus the frozen build's dev-round runs, at up to 3 families for each repository, and its file is committed with this one (TBD: path, sha256). If fewer repositories qualify than the design needs, the sizer runs again at the count reached. If no design passes there, the scored run does not start, and the size it needs is reported (prereg 215).
9. **Runners split by seed.** Each runner chunk holds every arm of one seed, so lockstep (prereg 120) holds inside it. Before each chunk, `C:/z0-runs/s7/box-check.sh` must pass (median bare launch at most 150 ms); `s3x-run.ps1` refuses to start otherwise.
10. **Hook voids.** After each chunk, `C:/z0-runs/s7/hook-check.mjs` reads every hippo start and prompt hook from the transcripts. A cancelled or timed-out one voids that position in every arm, through the drop list's `hookVoids` (`z0-filters.mjs`, `986d8f3b`). G4 fails when more than 5% of planned positions are hook-voided (`5a59ff5f`).
11. **Budgets.** The scored-session ceiling is the chosen design's count with every rule arm at 9 seeds (10,583 at 25 repositories on the stage 2 calibration), in place of the go's 9,500, since the cap rule can run every arm at 9. Authoring, probes, screens and dev rounds have their own budget of 3,000 sessions (TBD at the tag), counted in the record.
12. **Analyzer hash.** TBD: `docs/evals/z0-freeze/analyzer.sha256` for the analyzer at the tag, with the hook-void filter and the sizer fix.

## Limits, stated now

- **The teach messages stay scripted.** The paraphrase check covers capture's wording; it does not cover how a person corrects in a real session.
- **Dev families share the authoring process.** The dev rounds tune hippo on families written by the same blind briefs as the fresh ones, so a gain that depends on how those briefs phrase rules carries into the confirm.
