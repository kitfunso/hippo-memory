hippo logo, round 6

7 styles, every one rendered in three.js, each placed on a home screen, a site header, GitHub and a sticker. Memory dust · Sediment · Liquid chrome · Tiny world · Mascot · Half-life type · Holo card

Memory dust

Top pick. Reads instantly as a seahorse, and the logo itself fades like a memory.

Memory dust: a seahorse (hippocampus) sculpted from 120k additive mint particles on a swept-capsule SDF, dense and banded at the head and belly, its density halving along the spine so the tail thins into dust that drifts off with a few amber flecks.

Weak spot: the tail curl mostly dissolves, so the seahorse reads from the head, plates and fin rather than its signature spiral; alt.png pushes the dissolve further and loses more silhouette. The hippo-head particle variant was tried (renders/d02, d06) and killed as a mossy mound.

Sediment

Second pick. The decay law as an object; strongest concept, top needs work.

Sediment: a concrete, travertine, steel and smoked-glass core sample lit by one raking Ando sun blade.

Weak spot: the top slabs still read as stacked blocks rather than rock, and the mark loses its glow on transparent backgrounds because bloom is off there.

Liquid chrome

Album cover, not a logo. Turns into a lump at icon size.

Liquid chrome: a mirror-finish metaball hippo head, a drip still forming on its crown, floating over a black mirror in a mint studio.

Weak spot: the thin-film iridescence barely shows against the green env, and the crown's flat base betrays the marching-cubes plane, so it reads as a bucket at a glance.

Tiny world

The friendliest. Charm comes from the cube trail, the hippo is plain.

a chunky clay hippo on a floating sand tile, with a thought-trail of memory cubes rising from its head that glow mint while fresh and crumble into grey voxel dust as they drift away.

Weak spot: the hippo body is still a plain pill (no texture or pose) and the tile is bare beyond three stones, so the charm rides on the trail; the transparent mark is the whole diorama, which goes small on a README line.

Mascot

Real character, Docker-whale energy. Needs a sculpted mouth and more hippo muzzle.

a matte designer-toy hippo head sculpted from smooth-blended SDF primitives through MarchingCubes, wearing glowing mint round glasses (the alt balances a mint memory orb on its nose instead).

Weak spot: the smile is a painted tube line rather than a carved crease, so it sits flatter than a sculpted mouth and dips slightly at the centre; the orb alt can read as a clown nose from the front.

Half-life type

Best wordmark idea: read left to right and you have read the product.

the wordmark is the diagram: read it left to right and you have read the product. Palette bent: hero is ink type on a full mint field (glass has nothing to refract on ink); `alt.png` is the brief's version, glowing mint on ink; `icon.png` is the h alone with the decay running along its own extrusion depth.

Weak spot: the o loses its right rim and can read as `c` at small sizes; the wireframe stage is thin at 1024 and the frosted p is the least legible letter; the mint field has no print texture, so it can tip toward candy on a bright screen.

Holo card

Best launch/merch visual; the emblem alone is a generic seahorse.

the card's rarity tier is the memory's confidence: recall re-mints it, a wrong mark demotes it. Palette kept (ink body, mint and amber tints) but a rainbow underlay is mixed into the foil at 0.3 so the tints wash toward holographic rather than flat brand colour; `alt.png` is the single mint card at 2048, `icon.png` the foil seahorse cut onto plain ink (corners exact #0b0f0c), `mark.png` the same on transparent.

Weak spot: the seahorse is hand-drawn from circles and polygons and the head still sits a little stiff on the neck; the labels are small and turn to mush below 512 px; the two-card hero is a product shot rather than a logo, so the emblem alone has to carry the brand and it is a generic foil seahorse until the crack or the labels are in frame.
