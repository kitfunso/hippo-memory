**Name:** Half-life set in type. `hippo` in helvetiker bold, wall-mounted, low camera, hard sun; each letter is one stage of forgetting: h solid, i mint glass, p frosted, p wireframe, o noise-eaten into cubes that drift off the wall (custom dissolve shader in the physical material plus its shadow-depth material, so the eaten parts stop casting).
**Idea:** the wordmark is the diagram: read it left to right and you have read the product. Palette bent: hero is ink type on a full mint field (glass has nothing to refract on ink); `alt.png` is the brief's version, glowing mint on ink; `icon.png` is the h alone with the decay running along its own extrusion depth.
**Weak:** the o loses its right rim and can read as `c` at small sizes; the wireframe stage is thin at 1024 and the frosted p is the least legible letter; the mint field has no print texture, so it can tip toward candy on a bright screen.
