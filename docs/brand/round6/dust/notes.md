Memory dust: a seahorse (hippocampus) sculpted from 120k additive mint particles on a swept-capsule SDF, dense and banded at the head and belly, its density halving along the spine so the tail thins into dust that drifts off with a few amber flecks.
Weak: the tail curl mostly dissolves, so the seahorse reads from the head, plates and fin rather than its signature spiral; alt.png pushes the dissolve further and loses more silhouette. The hippo-head particle variant was tried (renders/d02, d06) and killed as a mossy mound.
Render: `python render_sd.py "dust/hero=dust/scene.html?size=2048&tx=0.15&aperture=0.03&half=0.6"` from round6; icon adds `dist=3.6&ty=0.15&down=1024`, mark adds `bg=black&key=1` (luminance key from a true-black render), alt uses `half=0.28&drift=0.7&far=0.15&t0=0.45`.
